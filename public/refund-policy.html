Refund Policy

We aim to resolve damaged, incorrect, or unavailable-item issues fairly and promptly.

Damaged or incorrect items

Contact support within 24 hours of delivery with your order code and a photo of the item. We will review the issue and confirm whether a replacement or refund is appropriate.

Unavailable items

If an item cannot be supplied, support will explain the available resolution before closing the order. We do not charge for products that are not supplied.

Refund timing

Approved refunds are returned through the original payment method where possible. Processing time depends on the payment provider. Cash on Delivery refunds are arranged by support after the issue is verified.

How to contact us

Use the Help & Support button on the store, choose Ask a question, and include your order code and relevant details.
