Privacy Policy

This policy explains the information ShakarGanj Grocery uses to process and support Lahore orders.

Information we collect

When you order or contact support, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, order details, and payment status. We do not store JazzCash wallet PINs or card details on our servers.

How we use it

We use this information to process orders, arrange delivery, provide support, maintain account access, and improve the store experience.

Sharing and retention

Information is shared only with service providers needed to process payments, deliver orders, or send configured support notifications. We retain order and support records for operational and accounting needs.

Your choices

For questions about your information or a support request, contact us through the Help & Support button and include your Form ID or order code.
